# University of Layyah - Complete Academic Programs & Faculties

The University of Layyah (UL) offers 62 academic degree programs across 5 main faculties and 22 departments. Below is the complete list of academic programs offered:

- **Department of Computer Science:**
  - BS Computer Science (BSCS)
  - BSCS - Artificial Intelligence (AI)
  - BSCS - Data Science
- **Department of Information Technology:**
  - BSCS - Information Technology (BS IT)
- **Department of Computer Engineering:**
  - B.Sc Computer Engineering
