# University of Layyah - Facilities, Scholarships, Hostel & Transport

## Transport Facility
- The university provides dedicated bus services covering multiple routes in Layyah city and surrounding areas:
  - Karor Lal Esan route
  - Kot Sultan route
  - Fatehpur route
  - Chowk Azam route
  - Layyah City central stops
- Transport office contact: Available during university hours for bus pass issuance.

## Hostel Accommodation
- University offers hostel accommodation for outstation students (Boys and Girls hostels) with 24/7 security, mess facility, and study rooms.
- Hostel details and application forms: https://ul.edu.pk/page/faculty-hostel

## Scholarships & Financial Assistance
- **PEEF Scholarships:** Punjab Educational Endowment Fund scholarships for needy and meritorious students.
- **Ehsaas Undergraduate Scholarship:** Full tuition fee waiver plus monthly stipend for deserving undergraduate students.
- **HEC Need-Based Scholarships:** Financial aid for underprivileged students.
- **University Merit Scholarships:** Semester-wise fee concessions for top position holders in each department.
- Details at: https://ul.edu.pk/page/Scholarships
