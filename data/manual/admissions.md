# University of Layyah - Admissions, How to Apply & Eligibility

## How to Apply for Admission
1. Visit the online admission portal: https://ul.edu.pk/page/How-to-Apply
2. Register an account with your CNIC/B-Form and active email address.
3. Fill in your personal details, academic qualifications (Matric, Intermediate/FSc/FA/DAE).
4. Select your desired degree program(s).
5. Generate and download the admission processing fee challan voucher from https://ul.edu.pk/challan.
6. Pay the challan at the designated bank branch (usually Bank of Punjab / HBL) or online.
7. Upload the paid fee voucher receipt on the portal and submit the application before the deadline.

## General Eligibility Criteria
- **BS Programs (4 Years):** Minimum 45% to 50% marks in Intermediate (FA / F.Sc / ICS / I.Com / DAE) or equivalent qualification from a recognized board.
- **BS Computer Science / AI / Data Science / IT:** F.Sc Pre-Engineering, ICS, or General Science with Mathematics (minimum 50% marks).
- **B.Sc Computer Engineering:** F.Sc Pre-Engineering with Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry (minimum 60% marks and entry test ECAT if applicable).
- **Post-ADP Programs (5th Semester):** 14 years of education (BA / B.Sc / ADP / Associate Degree) in relevant subject with at least 45% marks or 2.0 CGPA.
- **MS / MPhil Programs:** 16 years of education (BS 4-years or Master degree) with minimum 2.5 CGPA or 2nd Division, plus GAT General / University departmental test.

## Merit Lists & Selection
- Merit lists are published officially online at: https://ul.edu.pk/program_merit_list
- Selected candidates must submit original documents and semester fee before the due date to secure admission.
