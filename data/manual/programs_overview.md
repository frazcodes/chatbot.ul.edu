# University of Layyah - Complete List of Academic Programs Offered

The University of Layyah offers 62 academic degree programs across 5 faculties and 22 departments:

   - BSCS - Information Technology (BS IT)
   - B.Sc Computer Engineering

   - BS Sociology & Post-ADP Sociology

   - BS Public Administration & Governance & Post-ADP Public Administration
